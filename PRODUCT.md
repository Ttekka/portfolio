## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: the BTS SIO examination board (an SISR teacher and an IT professional) who read the portfolio before and during the E5 oral, on a laptop or a projected screen, to find each professional achievement (RP), its proof and the bloc 1 competences it covers. Secondary: recruiters and schools (licence professionnelle) looking at the candidate's background.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Gwendal Riquier, BTS SIO option SISR (session 2027, ESUP, alternance at Inetum for the client TDF). Success: an examiner reaches any RP page in two clicks, reads it without effort and can open the synthesis table and the proofs.

## Positioning
A dossier that maps every real achievement to the six bloc 1 competences, with the official synthesis table online and downloadable.

## Operating Context
Read during the E5 oral (10 min presentation + 30 min questions), alongside a slide deck. Accessed without any account, often in a classroom on a projector or a teacher's laptop.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.
- Pages: home (index.html), one page per RP in projects/, veille page, personal Minecraft server page (kept as a personal lab, separate from the RPs), mentions légales.
- Downloads in docs/: synthesis table (.xlsx) and the bristendal.local infrastructure documentation (PDF).
- The page URLs must not change (they are cited in the synthesis table).
